# Domain and UK Branding Strategy

## Recommended Domain

For a UK-focused business audience, use `.co.uk` as the primary domain where possible.

Also consider purchasing:

- `.uk`
- `.com`

Redirect secondary domains to the primary canonical domain to avoid duplicate content.

## Registrar

GoDaddy can be used as a registrar, but it should not be assumed to be the best application hosting provider for this stack.

## Brand Placeholders

Current placeholders:

- VenusCore
- VenusCore logo
- UK Phone Number
- Business Email
- UK Business Address
- Company Registration Number

Replace only with verified business details.
