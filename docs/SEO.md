# SEO Plan

- `sitemap.xml`
- `robots.txt`

- ProfessionalService JSON-LD
- Article-style metadata on news detail pages

## Content Rules

- Use UK English.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, certifications or statistics.
- Mark demo content as placeholder until replaced with verified material.
